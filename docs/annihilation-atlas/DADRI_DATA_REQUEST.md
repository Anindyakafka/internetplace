# Dadri Data and Archive Request

Keep original downloads unchanged under `research/annihilation-atlas/raw/dadri/` and private material under `research/annihilation-atlas/private/dadri/`. Both locations are excluded from Git. Add every source to `research/annihilation-atlas/DADRI_SOURCE_REGISTER.csv`.

## Priority A: first working packet

1. Census 1991, 2001, and 2011 District Census Handbooks, Primary Census Abstracts, Village/Town Directories, and available SC/ST, work, occupation, literacy, religion, housing, and amenities tables for Dadri/Gautam Buddha Nagar and predecessor geographies.
2. Matching village, town, sub-district, and district boundaries plus official location-code directories for each usable year.
3. Gautam Buddha Nagar district gazetteer and older gazetteers/settlement reports covering Dadri before the district existed.
4. Dadri Forecast material: manifesto, project pages, zines, event records, research notes, bibliographies, photographs, and a list of who owns each item and what may be republished.
5. Your private positionality note: what you did in the collective, when, with whom, which places you worked in, what may be public, and what must remain private.
6. Five to ten anti-caste, labour, agrarian, minority, ecological, or local-history texts that shaped the collective's work.

## Land, planning, and capital

- Revenue and settlement reports; village and cadastral maps where lawful and safe.
- Aggregated landholding and Agriculture Census tables.
- Land acquisition notifications, awards, rehabilitation policies, court decisions, and compensation rules.
- Dadri, Gautam Buddha Nagar, NCR, Greater Noida, and relevant industrial/development authority master plans and land-use plans.
- Infrastructure documents for rail, roads, freight, power, industrial areas, logistics, and urban expansion.
- Satellite-derived land-cover products with acquisition dates and methodology.

Do not place named ownership records, personal compensation records, or unredacted litigation files in the public repository.

## Labour and social reproduction

- Census worker classifications across available years.
- PLFS/NSS employment, migration, consumption, informal-sector, and employment-unemployment rounds with documentation. District estimates may not be statistically supportable; retain state/region identifiers and sampling information.
- Labour department inspections, factory/industry directories, occupational safety records, and wage notifications.
- Studies or records concerning agrarian labour, construction, industrial work, sanitation work, domestic work, transport, and migrant labour.
- School, health, housing, water, sanitation, and public-distribution records at a safe aggregate level.

## Caste, jati, communalism, and institutions

- Official SC/ST/OBC schedules and changes over time, with the issuing authority and date.
- Census aggregate SC/ST and religion tables; never infer jati from names or neighbourhoods.
- NCRB aggregate tables, police or commission reports, judgments, and verified reporting relevant to caste or communal violence.
- Documents about the killing of Mohammad Akhlaq and its aftermath, collected with attention to family privacy, contested accounts, and the difference between court records, reporting, and political narration.
- Anti-caste organisations, minority organisations, unions, pamphlets, statements, meetings, memorial practices, and oral histories where permission exists.

Recorded crime is evidence about reporting and institutions, not a direct measure of all violence.

## Ecology and unequal exposure

- Central and Uttar Pradesh pollution-control monitoring, consent, inspection, compliance, and environmental statement records.
- Environmental impact assessments, clearances, hearings, monitoring reports, and compliance reports for major projects.
- Central Ground Water Board aquifer, water-level, groundwater-quality, and assessment reports.
- Wetland, drainage, flood, waterbody, soil, land-cover, and air-quality datasets with dates and methods.
- Community accounts of water, pollution, agriculture, illness, animal life, and ecological change. Do not present these as clinical causal evidence unless appropriate research supports that claim.

## Counter-archive and lived history

- Dadri Forecast and Khandera Art Space materials.
- Field notes, sketches, maps, questionnaires, workshop plans, and correspondence.
- Oral histories with explicit terms for quotation, naming, audio, location, withdrawal, and web publication.
- Photographs with creator, date, place, depicted-person consent, copyright, and publication status.
- Local newspapers, pamphlets, poetry, songs, art, school work, union material, and community publications.
- A list of silences: refused interviews, destroyed records, inaccessible documents, unsafe details, and categories people reject.

## Metadata required for every item

- Stable source ID and exact title.
- Creator/producer and who classified the people or events described.
- Dates covered, publication date, and access date.
- Geographic coverage, boundary vintage, and observation unit.
- Original URL or physical archive reference.
- Licence/copyright and permission status.
- Original file path and documentation path.
- Known exclusions, disputes, translation needs, and safety restrictions.
- Proposed Atlas room: Map, Measure, File, Absence, or Struggle.
