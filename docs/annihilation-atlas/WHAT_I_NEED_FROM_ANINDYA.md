# What I Need From Anindya

## Decisions recorded for Prototype I

The initial direction is now set:

1. **First place:** Dadri, Gautam Buddha Nagar.
2. **Material scope:** Land, labour, caste/jati, communalism, ecology, state/capital, social reproduction, and resistance as connected domains.
3. **Period:** The contemporary condition understood through historical transition.
4. **Relationship:** Anindya was part of the Dadri Forecast collective. A fuller positionality and permissions note is still required.
5. **Languages:** Still to be answered. This means the languages in which Anindya can confidently read and evaluate a source without translation, for example Hindi, English, Bengali, or Urdu.
6. **Public voice:** Both first-person situated narration and institutional research prose, clearly distinguished.

The working research and collection plans are in `PROTOTYPE_I_DADRI_BRIEF.md` and `DADRI_DATA_REQUEST.md`.

## Files to collect first

Do not clean, rename, convert, or combine the originals. Put them in the matching local `raw/` folders and add a row to `DATA_INVENTORY.csv`.

### Required foundation

- Census 2011 Primary Census Abstract for Total, SC, and ST at the relevant geographic level.
- Relevant Census work, occupation, education, and housing tables.
- Administrative boundary files matching the data year, with official area codes.
- District Census Handbook and Village/Town Directory for the selected place.
- One PLFS annual unit-level release with every documentation and layout file.
- Relevant Agriculture Census tables if land or agrarian labour is selected.
- Relevant NCRB *Crime in India* tables and methodological notes if recorded atrocities are used.
- Official SC/ST/OBC schedules or notifications relevant to the place and period.

### Counter-archive

- A starter bibliography of 20–40 anti-caste books, articles, pamphlets, periodicals, speeches, judgments, movement documents, or oral histories relevant to the question.
- Local-language sources, including where they can be accessed.
- Names of archives, libraries, organizations, researchers, or organizers who may hold relevant material.
- For every non-public item: copyright status, owner/contact, and permission status.

### Your existing material

- Field notes and methodology notes, stored under `private/` until reviewed.
- Photographs with date, place, creator, depicted-person consent, and publication permission.
- Audio with participant consent terms and whether voice/name/location may be public.
- Existing maps, survey tools, codebooks, questionnaires, and correspondence about data access.
- A list of details that must never be made public.

## Access tasks only you can complete

- Register for and accept the terms governing NFHS/DHS microdata if we decide it is necessary.
- Obtain licensed or application-based survey data in your own name/institutional capacity.
- Request permission for private archives, movement documents, photographs, and recordings.
- Decide whether any fieldwork consent already obtained covers web publication; research participation alone does not automatically imply publication consent.
- Identify at least two people who could later review the project politically or substantively; no contact is needed yet.

## First handoff package

The minimum useful package is:

1. Your answers to the six decisions above.
2. Census PCA Total/SC/ST files for the chosen place.
3. A matching boundary file and code list.
4. The District Census Handbook.
5. Five to ten foundational counter-archive citations.
6. A private note describing your relationship to the place and foreseeable risks.

With that package, we can build the source audit, boundary concordance, first “absence” register, and a non-public prototype before adding more datasets.
